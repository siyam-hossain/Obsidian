# Types of Statistics

There are two type of statistics

1. Descriptive
2. Inferential

---

**Descriptive:**
It consist of,

- Organizing
- and summarizing of data

Technique it uses

1. Measure of central tendency
    - Mean
    - Median
    - Mode
2. Measure of Dispersion
    - Variance
    - Standard deviation

---

**Inferential:**
Collect data {sample data} --> Conclusion or Inferences {using some experiment} --> other data {population data}

```text
population data > sample data
```
